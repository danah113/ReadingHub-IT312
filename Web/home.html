Featured offerings

Three ways to get more out of your reading.

Mystery Night

Join us for a weekly evening of gripping whodunits. We discuss clues, twists and theories over snacks, so bring your detective hat.

Poetry Workshop

Write, share and get friendly feedback in a relaxed group. All levels are welcome, from first-time writers to regular poets.

Kids Story Hour

Read-aloud stories and a fun craft for young readers aged 4 to 9. It’s a gentle way to build a love of books early.

Book of this month

Before the coffee gets cold

Is a 2015 novel by Toshikazu Kawaguchi. It tells the story of a café in Tokyo that allows its customers to travel back in time, as long as they return before their coffee gets cold.
